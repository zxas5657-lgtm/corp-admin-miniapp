## 1. `pages/vehicles/index` (tab)
- Scope segmented control from `view.scopes`. Each segment's hit area is at least 44px. The selected scope lives in page data and resets to the role default on role change.
- Status-grouped list:
  - a `section-label` per group heading
  - cards with title, `status-pill`, `whenText`, destination and applicant
  - the card navigates to `/pages/vehicle-detail/index?id=…`
- Empty state from `view.empty`. Its action opens the form when present.
- The bottom `primary-button` comes from `view.requestButton`.
- **Badge:** on `show` of **every tab page** and on store change, call `getTabBar().setBadge(2, view.badgeCount)`.
  - Do this through one small helper, `utils/badge.js` `refreshVehicleBadge(page)`, that computes `vehicleBadgeCount(role, personId, state.vehicles)`. Don't build the whole list view on other tabs.
  - Wire the helper into the schedule, meetings and me pages too.

## 2. `pages/vehicle-detail/index` (sub-page; register it)
- Not-found state.
- Title and `status-pill`.
- **Step bar** from `view.steps`: three dots joined by a line.
  - done is ok-green, current is primary, todo is `--icon` grey.
  - Labels sit under the dots, and `timeText` sits under the labels in 12px.
  - When `steps` is null, show `view.rejectBanner` as a red notice when present.
- `cell-row` group from `view.cells`. The note is stacked.
- `view.warnings` as small warn-tone notices.
- **Actions** from `view.actions`:
  - the primary action as the fixed `primary-button`
  - secondary and danger actions as text buttons above it
  - The actions are:
    - `dispatch` and `reassign` open the **dispatch sheet**.
    - `reject` opens the **reject sheet**.
    - `complete` opens a confirm sheet.
    - `withdraw` and `cancelDispatch` open a danger confirm sheet.
- **Dispatch sheet** (`dispatchSheetView` with `selectedCarId`):
  - radio rows per car, showing the label, `seatsText`, and `busyText` when the car is busy (the row is disabled)
  - the seat warning shown inline
  - confirm calls `transition(vehicle, 'dispatch' | 'reassign', { carId }, { role, personId, now, drivers: DRIVERS, vehicles: state.vehicles })`, then `store.updateVehicle`
- **Reject sheet:** a `textarea` for the reason. An empty reason gives an inline error from `error.vehicle.reject.reasonRequired`.
- **On `ok: false`:** show `notice-bar` error with `t('error.' + code)` and keep the sheet open for validation errors. For `vehicle.alreadyHandled`, close the sheet and re-render (review P1-5: stale state is never silent).
- **On a storage failure:** a `storage.writeFailed` notice, and the record is unchanged.
- **On success:** toast (we stay on the page) and re-render.
